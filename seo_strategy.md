### 2.1 Title Tags (Change These Immediately)

Each page needs a unique, keyword-rich title. Currently your homepage says:
`LKTaxi - Trusted Taxi Service in Sri Lanka | Airport Transfers & Tours`

Change to:
`Sri Lanka Taxi Booking | Airport Transfers, Tours & Yala Safari | LKTaxi`

**Other pages:**

- Safari page: `Yala Safari Booking Sri Lanka — Private Jeep Tours from Colombo | LKTaxi`
- Tours page: `Sri Lanka Private Taxi Tours | Colombo, Galle, Ella, Kandy | LKTaxi`
- Airport page: `Colombo Airport Taxi | BIA Arrivals & Transfers | LKTaxi`
- Blog index: `Sri Lanka Travel Blog — Safari, Taxi Tips & Destination Guides | LKTaxi`

### 2.2 Meta Descriptions (160 characters max, must include a call to action)

Homepage: "Book safe, affordable taxis and Yala safari jeeps in Sri Lanka. Airport transfers, day tours & wildlife packages for foreign tourists. Book online now."

### 2.3 Heading Structure (H1, H2, H3)

Every page must have ONE H1 that contains your main keyword. Then use H2s for subtopics. Google reads these as your page's outline.

**Homepage H1:** "Book Sri Lanka Taxis & Yala Safari Tours Online"

**Safari page H1:** "Yala National Park Safari Booking — Private Jeep Tours"

### 2.4 Image Alt Text

Every image on your site must have descriptive alt text with keywords:
- ❌ `alt="img1.jpg"`
- ✅ `alt="Private taxi van from Colombo to Yala National Park, Sri Lanka"`

### 2.5 URL Structure

Clean, keyword-based URLs rank better:
- ❌ `/page?id=12`

- ✅ `/colombo-airport-taxi`

## PART 3: TECHNICAL SEO — The Foundation

### 3.1 Google Search Console (Do This Today)
1. Go to search.google.com/search-console
2. Add your site and verify ownership
3. Submit your sitemap (lktaxi.com/sitemap.xml)
4. Check for crawl errors and fix them

### 3.2 Google Business Profile (Free — Critical for Local SEO)
1. Go to business.google.com
2. Create a profile for "LKTaxi"
3. Category: "Taxi service" + "Tour operator"
4. Add: address, phone, website, photos, opening hours
5. Write a keyword-rich description
6. This gets you into Google Maps and the local pack

### 3.3 Site Speed (Google's Ranking Factor)
- Test your speed at pagespeed.web.dev
- If score is below 70, compress all images (use squoosh.app — free)
- Enable browser caching on your server
- Use WebP image format instead of JPG/PNG

### 3.4 Mobile Optimisation
- Over 75% of tourist searches are on mobile
- Test at search.google.com/test/mobile-friendly
- Fix any issues immediately — Google uses mobile-first indexing

### 3.5 Schema Markup (Structured Data)
Add this JSON-LD code to your homepage `<head>`. It tells Google exactly what your business is:

```json
{
  "@context": "https://schema.org",
  "@type": "TaxiService",
  "name": "LKTaxi",
  "url": "https://www.lktaxi.com",
  "telephone": "+94-YOUR-NUMBER",
  "description": "Safe and reliable taxi and safari booking service in Sri Lanka for foreign tourists.",
  "areaServed": "Sri Lanka",
  "serviceType": ["Airport Transfer", "Yala Safari Booking", "Private Tours"],
  "priceRange": "$$",
  "image": "https://www.lktaxi.com/your-logo.jpg"
}
```

Add this to your Yala Safari page:

```json
{
  "@context": "https://schema.org",
  "@type": "TouristAttraction",
  "name": "Yala Safari Booking — LKTaxi",
  "description": "Book private Yala safari jeep tours from Colombo with expert drivers.",
  "url": "https://www.lktaxi.com/yala-safari-booking"
}
```

## PART 5: BACKLINK BUILDING — The #1 Ranking Factor

Google ranks sites higher when other trusted websites link to them. Here's how to get links:

### Quick Wins (Do This Month)

**1. TripAdvisor Listing**
- Create a free listing at tripadvisor.com
- Category: Tours & Activities / Private Transportation
- Link back to lktaxi.com
- Encourage customers to leave reviews here

**2. Lonely Planet / Wikitravel Mentions**
- Edit Sri Lanka transport pages to mention your service where appropriate
- These are high-authority sites

**3. Sri Lanka Tourism Board**
- Register at sltda.gov.lk (Sri Lanka Tourism Development Authority)
- Being a registered operator boosts trust and can earn a backlink

**4. Travel Blogs & Influencers**
- Search Google for "Sri Lanka travel blog" + "Yala safari"
- Email the top 20 bloggers offering a free safari ride in exchange for an honest review with a link to your site
- This is called "blogger outreach" and it's extremely effective

**5. Guest Posts**
- Write articles for other Sri Lanka travel websites
- In your author bio, link back to lktaxi.com
- Target sites like: srilankainstyle.com, thesrilankatravel.com

**6. Google Reviews (Critical)**
- After every trip, WhatsApp your customers a direct link to leave a Google Review
- Aim for 50+ reviews with 4.8+ rating
- Respond to every review — Google sees this as active business engagement
